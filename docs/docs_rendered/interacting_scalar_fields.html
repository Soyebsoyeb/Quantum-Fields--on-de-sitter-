Interacting scalar field

Status: analytic derivations; symbolic check of Hint​ and dimensions (interactions.py).

Sint​=−∫d4x−g​4!λ​ϕ4.

Dimension. [d4x]=−4, [ϕ]=1 ⇒[λ]=0: λϕ4 is classically marginal in d=4. (Quantum mechanically, λ runs and ϕ4 needs counterterms; see renormalization.md.)

Interaction Hamiltonian. The interaction contains no derivatives so πϕ​=a2ϕ′ is unchanged by it, and the Legendre transform gives Hint​=−Lint​=a44!λ​ϕ4 (conformal time, density per unit comoving volume). Verified symbolically (interaction_hamiltonian_density). In terms of χ=aϕ:

Hint​=4!λ​χ4,
explicitly time-independent for any m,ξ: all time dependence of the interaction sits in the mode functions vk​.

Free vs interacting evolution. Write χ^​H​(η)=U†(η,η0​)χ^​I​(η)U(η,η0​), U=Texp[−i∫η0​η​HI​], with χ^​I​ the free field built from vk​. Perturbation theory is an expansion in λ around a specified initial state ∣Ω⟩ (here the free BD vacuum, switched on adiabatically in the infinite past). Different initial states or η0​ give different perturbative series; the "interacting vacuum" is not unique and may not exist on the whole patch (IR/secular issues).

Conformal special case. m=0,ξ=1/6: ν=1/2, v=e−ikη/2k​, the problem is flat-space χ4 theory on η∈(−∞,0); one expects (and we find) no secular growth.
