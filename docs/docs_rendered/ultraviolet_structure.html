Short-distance form

With u=(1−Z)/2=sin2(Hσ/2) (σ geodesic distance), the closed-form G+ has, from the 2​F1​ connection formula with c=a+b−1 (DLMF 15.8.10),

G+=uA​+Blnu+C+O(ulnu),A=16π2H2​, B=(49​−ν2−2)A, C=B[ψ(23​+ν)+ψ(23​−ν)+2γE​−1].
The Frobenius recursion of the invariant ODE independently gives B=(ab−2)A — the same value. Numerically the residual G−(A/u+Blnu+C) scales like ulnu (Fig. 6b) for ν=1,1.3,0.8i (for ν=1/2, B=C=0 and the residual is round-off). Because u≃H2σ2/4, A/u→1/(4π2σ2): the leading singularity is the flat-space one (Fig. 6a: 4π2d2G+→1, d = proper distance), independent of m,ξ and the (BD) state. The lnu coefficient B is local geometry/mass data (m2+(ξ−61​)R times H2/16π2-type terms).

Hadamard condition

A state is Hadamard if

G+(x,x′)=8π21​[σ2Δ1/2​+Vlnσ2+W],  σ2→σ2+iϵ(t−t′)+…
with Δ1/2 the Van Vleck–Morette determinant and V determined locally by the geometry and m,ξ (via Hadamard recursion); only the smooth W carries state information. Equivalent microlocal form: wavefront set condition (Radzikowski 1996). Hadamard states are those for which ⟨Tμν​⟩ and Wick polynomials can be consistently defined (Wald 1977; Hollands–Wald 2001). BD satisfies it (the structure above, with V↔B), while generic α-vacua do not (antipodal singularity). Status: the expansion above is checked; the full Hadamard recursion and the wavefront-set statement are not implemented, only described.

Cutoffs are not renormalisation

Fig. 6c: with a sharp comoving cutoff Λ, ⟨ϕ2⟩Λ​≃4π2H2​[lnkIR​Λ​+2η2Λ2​] (ν=3/2, exact): a quadratic divergence ∝(Λ/a)2 in physical units plus a log. A comoving cutoff breaks de Sitter invariance and time-translation covariance (physical cutoff Λ/a changes with time), and the finite remainder depends on the cutoff shape. It is a diagnostic of the divergence structure only; no counterterm, renormalisation condition or scheme is attached to it.
