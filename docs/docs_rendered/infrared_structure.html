Massless vs light massive

What is and is not claimed

An IR-divergent intermediate expression (∫dk/k with a formal cutoff) is not a demonstrably ill-defined physical prediction: observables insensitive to the zero mode (derivative correlators, ⟨ϕ(x)−ϕ(x′)⟩2) are IR finite at ν=3/2; the dependence on kIR​ in ⟨ϕ2⟩ reflects the dependence on the state/initial patch size. Conversely, the absence of an SO(1,4)-invariant state at ν=3/2 is a theorem-level statement (Allen–Folacci 1987), not an artefact of the cutoff. Interacting IR behaviour: Starobinsky 1986; Starobinsky–Yokoyama 1994; Marolf–Morrison 2011; Hollands 2013; Polyakov 2012 (not endorsed here as settled).
