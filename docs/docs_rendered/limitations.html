Limitations

Honest scope statement; every item is either a stated assumption or an unimplemented extension.

Scope. Fixed four-dimensional de Sitter, spatially flat chart only. Free scalar: fully implemented and validated for ν real and imaginary. Interactions: first-order, tree-level contact diagram (the conformal case analytically, the massless minimal case with its log). No loops, no second order, no exchange diagrams.

Not implemented / only described: α-vacua; unequal-time position-space Wightman via mode sum (the closed form is available); position-space Feynman propagator; the global or static chart; Hadamard recursion and a covariant renormalised ⟨ϕ2⟩ or ⟨Tμν​⟩; loop corrections, running of λ, stochastic resummation beyond the equilibrium benchmark; scalar and vector metric perturbations, graviton interactions, ghosts.

Numerical. Double precision Hankel functions at late times lose ∼(k∣η∣)−3 digits in the commutator combination; mpmath is used where this matters and scipy results are only trusted away from η→0 (a dedicated test cross-checks both). The ODE Wronskian residual grows with ∣v∣∣v′∣. Figures show numerics, which validate (they do not prove) the stated analytic results.

Physics caveats. (i) The BD state is a statement about the chart boundary, not an initial condition on a complete Cauchy surface. (ii) For ν≥3/2 the BD two-point function is IR divergent; results with a cutoff kIR​ depend on it. (iii) Point-splitting constants are scheme-dependent. (iv) The first-order secular growth signals breakdown of the fixed-order expansion, not an inconsistency of the theory. (v) The tensor action normalisation is quoted from the literature (standard) and validated by reproducing Δt2​=2H2/π2Mp2​ numerically, not rederived symbolically from the Einstein–Hilbert action.
