de Sitter geometry

Status: analytic result + symbolic verification (backgrounds.py, tests/test_curvature.py).

Embedding

dS4​ is the hyperboloid −X02​+X12​+X22​+X32​+X42​=H−2 in R1,4 with the induced metric. It is maximally symmetric (SO(1,4)), Rμνρσ​=H2(gμρ​gνσ​−gμσ​gνρ​), hence Rμν​=3H2gμν​ and R=12H2.

The invariant Z(x,x′)=H2X⋅X′ equals cos(Hσ) for spacelike geodesic separation σ, Z=1 at coincidence, Z=−1 at the antipodal point (and light-cone of the antipode), and Z>1 for timelike separation.

The flat chart is obtained by X0​=H1​sinhHt+2H​eHtx2, X4​=H1​coshHt−2H​eHtx2, Xi​=eHtxi​ (one checks X42​−X02​=H−2−e2Htx2). It is geodesically incomplete to the past: η→−∞ is a coordinate horizon, not a past boundary of the manifold. A "state defined at η→−∞" is therefore a statement about the behaviour of modes near the boundary of the patch, not an initial condition on a Cauchy surface of the full spacetime.

In the flat chart Z=2ηη′η2+η′2−r2​, r=∣x−x′∣ (this expression is tested: the independent mode-sum and the closed form agree to 10−13 only if it is correct, tests/test_two_point.py).

Horizon crossing

A mode with comoving wavenumber k has physical wavenumber k/a; k=aH⟺k∣η∣=1. The comoving Hubble radius is 1/(aH)=−η, shrinking in time, so every mode eventually leaves the horizon (Fig. 1).
