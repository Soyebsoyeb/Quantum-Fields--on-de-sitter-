Scalar-field action and field equation

Status: analytic derivation + symbolic verification (scalar_action.py).

S[ϕ]=−21​∫d4x−g​[gμν∇μ​ϕ∇ν​ϕ+(m2+ξR)ϕ2].

Variation

Let ϕ→ϕ+δϕ with δϕ=0 on the initial/final hypersurfaces and δϕ→0 at spatial infinity.

δS=−∫d4x−g​[∇μϕ∇μ​δϕ+(m2+ξR)ϕδϕ].
Use −g​∇μϕ∇μ​δϕ=∂μ​(−g​δϕ∇μϕ)−δϕ∂μ​(−g​∇μϕ) (product rule), and −g​1​∂μ​(−g​∇μϕ)=□ϕ (definition). The total derivative integrates to a boundary term ∮dΣμ​δϕ∇μϕ which vanishes under the stated boundary assumptions. Hence
δS=∫d4x−g​δϕ[□ϕ−(m2+ξR)ϕ],
and stationarity for arbitrary δϕ gives (□−m2−ξR)ϕ=0.

Independent verification

euler_lagrange_vs_klein_gordon() builds L for a generic conformally flat FRW metric a(η), applies SymPy's Euler–Lagrange operator and verifies

∂ϕ∂L​−∂μ​∂(∂μ​ϕ)∂L​=−g​(□−m2−ξR)ϕ
with −g​=a4 (a>0) and R computed from the Riemann convention in conventions.md (R=6a′′/a3). The difference simplifies to zero.
